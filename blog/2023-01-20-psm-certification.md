## About

Certification `Professional Scrum Master™ I`, also known as `PSM I`.

The definition of certification is as follow:

> The Professional Scrum Master™ I (PSM I) certification validates your knowledge of the Scrum framework, the Scrum Master accountabilities and how to apply Scrum. 

## Tips

Some materials used in the study for the certification:
- Primary resource: `Scrum Guide`.
- Primary Course: [Udemy - Formação total em Scrum](https://www.udemy.com/course/formacao-total-em-scrum) (pt-br).
- Other Course: [Alura](https://www.alura.com.br)'s Scrum courses in Agile training (pt-br).
- Simulations: [Udemy - Simulados para certificações Scrum Master](https://www.udemy.com/course/simulados-para-certificacoes-scrum-master) (pt-br).
- Other resources: Read a good part of the articles on the certification website as study recommendations, the "Scrum Master Learning Path".

See my certification badge [here](https://www.credly.com/badges/572249e3-f991-4198-948d-3c3d61b06c8c/public_url).
